How to play · Arcade house rules

Roll six dice. Select at least one scoring die or combination. Bank to save your turn points, or roll the remaining dice. A roll with no scoring dice is a Farkle: you lose all points from that turn.

  • Single 1: 100. Single 5: 50.
  • Three 1s: 1,000. Three of any other number: that number × 100.
  • Four, five, or six of a kind: double the triple score for each extra die.
  • A six-die straight or three pairs: 1,500.
  • Combinations must come from the same roll. Every selected die must score.
  • Score all available dice for “hot dice,” then roll all six again.
  • No minimum opening score. Once someone reaches the target, every other player gets one final turn. Highest score wins; ties favor the first player to reach that score.
